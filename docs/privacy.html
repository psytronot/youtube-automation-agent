Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

PSYTRONOT YouTube Automation is a personal application used by the channel owner to create, manage, analyze, and publish YouTube content.

Information accessed

When you authorize this application with Google, it may access information associated with the authorized YouTube channel, including channel information, video information, YouTube analytics information, and permissions required to upload and manage videos.

How information is used

Information accessed through the YouTube API is used only to operate and manage the authorized YouTube channel, including creating, uploading, scheduling, and analyzing content.

Data sharing

The application does not sell or publicly distribute personal information obtained through Google or YouTube APIs.

Data storage

Authentication credentials and application data are stored locally by the application where applicable and are used to provide the application's functionality.

Third-party services

This application uses Google services, including YouTube APIs and Google authentication. Use of those services is also subject to Google's applicable policies.
